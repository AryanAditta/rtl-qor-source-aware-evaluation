# Third-party and redistribution notice

This repository may reference external RTL sources, open-source EDA tools, libraries and published methods. Their original licenses and terms remain controlling.

The public release intentionally avoids redistributing third-party source RTL or dependencies unless redistribution permission is clear. Where source files are omitted, compact prediction/label tables, identifiers and provenance records may still be released so that manuscript-level numerical claims can be checked without republishing the underlying third-party code.

Referenced external tools and methods include Yosys/ABC, OpenSTA, the Nangate45 library and the released MasterRTL implementation. Inclusion of an identifier, metric or derived feature does not relicense the corresponding upstream project.

A final repository-wide license should be added only after author/supervisor approval and after confirming that its scope does not conflict with any redistributed third-party material.
